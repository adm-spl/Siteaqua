The Thermal Springs of São Pedro do Sul are known for their natural sulphurous waters. Our homes are a few minutes' walk away.

## What to visit
- Thermal baths (simulated hours and prices)
- Boardwalks along the Vouga river
- Mountain viewpoints

## Tip
Book thermal treatments in advance in high season.
